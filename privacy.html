Ember privacy policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

What Ember stores

Your liked songs, playlists (names and covers), downloads list and recently played songs. They are stored on your own devices. Ember has no servers of its own and its developer never receives your data.

Google sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google, Ember asks for two permissions:

  • Your email address, only to show which account you're signed in with.
  • Ember's own app folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata), to save one file with your library so your devices stay in sync. Ember cannot see, read or change any of your other Drive files.

This data goes only between your devices and Google. It is not sold, shared, used for ads, or used for anything other than syncing your library.

Ember's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Sign out in Ember's Settings, or remove Ember's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling Ember removes its data from your devices.
